## Overview

ArticleSong is a browser extension that transforms web articles into songs using AI-powered lyrics and music generation. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the extension works.

**TL;DR: We collect nothing. All data stays on your device. You control everything.**

## Data Collection

**ArticleSong does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage statistics, or analytics.**

We do not:
- Track which websites you visit
- Monitor which articles you convert to songs
- Collect any personally identifiable information
- Use analytics or telemetry services
- Send data to any servers we control (we don't have any servers)

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device** using your browser's built-in storage APIs:

### API Keys
- **Storage location:** `browser.storage.sync` (encrypted and managed by your browser)
- **What's stored:** Your SunoAPI key (required) and optionally your Anthropic API key
- **Who has access:** Only you, on devices where you're signed into your browser
- **Sync:** May sync across your devices via your browser's sync feature (controlled by you)

### Song History
- **Storage location:** `browser.storage.local` (stays on this device only)
- **What's stored:** List of generated songs with titles, URLs, timestamps, and lyrics
- **Who has access:** Only you, only on this device
- **Retention:** Up to your last 20 completed songs (configurable)

### Settings & Preferences
- **Storage location:** `browser.storage.local`
- **What's stored:** Your preferences (auto-download, notifications, etc.)
- **Who has access:** Only you, only on this device

### Custom Song Styles
- **Storage location:** `browser.storage.local`
- **What's stored:** Your 6 customized song style names and descriptions
- **Who has access:** Only you, only on this device
- **Default:** Comes with 6 pre-configured styles that you can customize
- **Usage:** These descriptions are sent to AI services (Anthropic or SunoAPI) along with article text when generating lyrics

You can clear all stored data at any time by:
1. Opening your browser's extension settings
2. Removing ArticleSong
3. Or using your browser's "Clear browsing data" feature

## Data Transmission

ArticleSong operates **entirely client-side** in your browser. We do not operate any backend servers.

The extension **only** transmits data to third-party APIs. Here's the complete flow:

### The Technical Flow

**Article Text → Lyrics Generation → SunoAPI → Suno.ai → Your Song**

### 1. SunoAPI (REQUIRED)
- **URL:** `https://api.sunoapi.org/api/v1/generate`
- **Purpose:** Intermediary service that forwards lyrics to Suno.ai for music generation
- **Data sent:**
  - Generated lyrics (from article text or from Anthropic)
  - Music style tags
  - Song title
  - Your SunoAPI key (in request headers)
- **When:** Every time you generate a song (required for all song generation)
- **Note:** SunoAPI acts as a middleman service between you and Suno.ai
- **Your responsibility:** Review SunoAPI's terms of service and privacy policy

### 2. Anthropic Claude API (OPTIONAL)
- **URL:** `https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages`
- **Purpose:** Generate enhanced, higher-quality song lyrics from article text
- **Data sent:** 
  - Article text extracted from the webpage
  - Your Anthropic API key (in request headers)
  - Model selection and prompt parameters
- **When:** Only if you configure an Anthropic API key
- **If not configured:** Lyrics are generated by SunoAPI directly instead
- **Your responsibility:** Review [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)

### Important Notes
- **SunoAPI is required** - All songs go through this intermediary service to reach Suno.ai
- **Anthropic is optional** - Enhances lyrics quality but not necessary for basic functionality
- These API providers have their own privacy policies and terms of service
- You are responsible for reviewing and accepting their terms
- ArticleSong acts only as a client making API requests on your behalf
- We have no control over how these services handle your data
- SunoAPI forwards your requests to Suno.ai (the actual music generation service)

## Permissions Explained

Browser extensions must declare permissions. Here's why ArticleSong needs each one:

### `activeTab`
**Why:** Read the current tab's content when you click the extension icon  
**Usage:** Extract article text from the page you're viewing  
**Limitation:** Only active when you explicitly click the extension

### `storage`
**Why:** Save your API keys, settings, and song history  
**Usage:** Store data locally in your browser  
**Limitation:** All data stays on your device

### `downloads`
**Why:** Save generated MP3 files  
**Usage:** Download completed songs to your Downloads folder  
**Limitation:** Only downloads files you explicitly request

### `notifications`
**Why:** Alert you when song generation completes  
**Usage:** Show browser notifications (can be disabled in settings)  
**Limitation:** Only shows status of your song requests

### `menus`
**Why:** Add context menu items  
**Usage:** Show different song style options when you click the extension icon  
**Limitation:** Menu items only appear on the extension icon

### `https://api.anthropic.com/*`
**Why:** (Optional) Make requests to Claude API  
**Usage:** Generate enhanced song lyrics from article text  
**Limitation:** Only communicates with this specific API endpoint  
**Optional:** Extension works without this if Anthropic API key is not configured

### `https://api.sunoapi.org/*`
**Why:** (Required) Make requests to SunoAPI  
**Usage:** Send lyrics to SunoAPI, which forwards them to Suno.ai for music generation  
**Limitation:** Only communicates with this specific API endpoint  
**Required:** All song generation flows through this intermediary service

### Content Script Access (`<all_urls>`)
**Why:** Extract article content from any webpage  
**Usage:** Use Mozilla's Readability.js library to parse article text  
**Limitation:** Only activates when you explicitly click the extension icon  
**Important:** We do NOT make network requests to arbitrary websites. This permission is only for reading page content locally in your browser.

## Third-Party Services

ArticleSong uses third-party services for song generation:

### SunoAPI (REQUIRED)
- **Role:** Intermediary service that forwards lyrics to Suno.ai for music generation
- Website: https://www.sunoapi.org (or similar - check for current endpoint)
- **Required:** Yes - all songs are generated through this service
- **What it does:** Takes lyrics and sends them to Suno.ai, then returns the generated music to you
- Pricing varies by plan
- You must provide your own API key
- You must review and accept their terms independently

### Anthropic Claude API (OPTIONAL)
- **Role:** Enhanced lyrics generation from article text
- Website: https://www.anthropic.com
- Privacy Policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- **Required:** No - you can use SunoAPI's lyrics generation instead
- **What it does:** Generates higher-quality, more creative lyrics from article text
- Cost: Pay-per-use (approximately $0.10 per song for lyrics generation)
- You must provide your own API key if you want to use this feature

### The Complete Flow
1. Article text is extracted from webpage (local)
2. Lyrics are generated (either by SunoAPI or by Anthropic if configured)
3. Lyrics are sent to SunoAPI
4. SunoAPI forwards to Suno.ai for music generation
5. Music is returned to your browser

**Important:** We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or responsible for these third-party services. You use them at your own discretion and risk.
